## Summary

**The addiction cycle works in real play.** Every step the release set out to fix passed live on the GM client: a failed save addicts; repeat doses don't stack addictions or highs and tolerance weakens the high in whole numbers; the Long Rest lists the addicted, relapses on a failed Wisdom check, starts withdrawal on a failed Constitution save, holds while in withdrawal, and forces the save with no doses left; a dose during withdrawal cancels it but keeps the addiction; expiry, Remove Withdrawal and deleting the last withdrawal effect all end the addiction exactly once (batch deletes included); the last dose leaves the drug at 0, a use at 0 is blocked, and a compendium drop refills it; +N, advantage and auto-pass all reach the dice, daily uses are spent and mismatched gear grants nothing; the character's own advantage still applies; the Remove macros remove only what they name; tolerance fades at rest for drugs the character isn't addicted to; a relapse dose skips the gear prompt without leaking the pass; drag-to-inventory and Simulate Dose seed state the way play does; no deprecated call has this module as its direct caller.
