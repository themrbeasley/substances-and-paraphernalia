# Substances and Paraphernalia: Wiki

Welcome to the GM Guide for the **Substances and Paraphernalia** Foundry VTT module. This wiki documents how the module works, how to author custom content, and how the moving parts fit together.

> The in-world journal that ships with the module is a one-page pointer back to this wiki. Updates land here between module releases.

## What the module does

- **Illicit substances and gear.** 18 drugs in three categories (stimulant, mind-altering, performance enhancer) across Fantasy, Sci-Fi and Modern settings, plus the paraphernalia that goes with them. Every high and every addiction penalty is a real effect, and the item text says exactly what is automated.
- **A gate on gear.** A drug can't be taken without ready gear for how it is taken, and single-use gear (Rolling Papers) gets used up.
- **An addiction loop.** Each dose can addict (Wisdom saving throw for mind-altering drugs, Constitution for the rest), tolerance weakens repeat doses, and a Long Rest asks the character to dose or abstain in the **Withdrawal Choices** window.
- **Withdrawal with teeth.** Abstaining always leads to withdrawal; a passed Constitution saving throw halves it. In combat it deals damage at the start of each of the character's turns for mind-altering drugs and performance enhancers.
- **Overdose that hurts.** A dose at the tolerance limit, or one taken while the drug's high is still on you, can overdose, with damage and a condition.
- **Dosing others.** Every drug has an activity for spiking a drink, blowing it in a face, jabbing a needle, slapping on a patch, or firing a dart. Stellar Mist can be thrown as a lingering gas bomb.
- **Save bypass gear.** Paraphernalia can help against the addiction save.
