# v0.10.0: dose others, withdrawal with teeth, and the content pass

**Status:** scope approved 2026-10-02; this spec awaits review.
**Goal:** make the module worth a real table test. Drugs can be forced on other creatures (spiked drinks, darts, gas clouds). Withdrawal and overdose hurt. Every drug does what its text says. The release carries a content warning and a guard that keeps the changelog and roadmap current.
**Version:** v0.10.0, a release candidate. After two or three real sessions with no surprises, v1.0 is stamped with no code changes. The module is hosted on GitHub only and will never go on the Foundry package registry.

**Sources:** the content audit and the Midi-QoL research of 2026-10-02 (Midi-QoL 14.0.12, DAE 14.0.14, dnd5e 5.3.3, Foundry V14 build 368; every key below was checked against that source). Decision ids (D1 to D17) are what the plan points at.

Each decision says what changes at the table, then how.

## Part 1: Automation

### D1. Dose someone else

**At the table.** A rogue slips Whisperdust into the duke's wine, a smuggler blows Coalshade into a guard's face, a medic jabs a soldier with Combat Cocktail. Whoever is dosed gets the whole dose: the high, the addiction save, tolerance, and the overdose check. The user isn't dosed. Every drug gets an activity for this (D10 lists them).

**How.**
- **The dose marker.** Each drug carries one extra effect, the *dose marker*: role `dose`, no changes, no statuses, not transferred. A dose-others activity lists the marker as its effect. Whatever puts the marker on a creature (Midi-QoL after a failed save or a hit, or after a use with targets; dnd5e's Apply button on the chat card), the module cancels the marker and runs the dose on that creature instead. Reason: the module doesn't need to know how the dose landed, so saves, attacks and plain targeted uses all work, with or without Midi.
- **Which drug.** The module finds the drug from the activity Midi records on the effect (`flags.dae.activity`), else from the effect's origin. Reason: when the thrower is concentrating, Midi sets the origin to the concentration effect.
- **Where it runs.** On the client that creates the marker. For a creature the user doesn't own, DAE creates it on the GM's client, so the GM's client runs the dose, including the addiction save window. The listener cancels synchronously and starts the dose without waiting, because Foundry only cancels on an immediate `false`.
- **The dosed creature's copy.** Tolerance, withdrawal and the Long Rest all key on the creature's own copy of the drug, and the Long Rest only lists drugs the creature holds. So the dose runs against their copy: the same item by id, else a drug with the same name, else the module gives them an empty copy (0 doses, same id when free).
- **The user isn't dosed.** An activity that lists the dose marker doses its targets, not its user; the post-use listener skips it. Every other activity on a drug still doses the user, as today. The Long Rest relapse uses the first activity that doses the user, not simply the first activity.
- **No gear bonuses on a forced dose.** The dosed creature's own save-bypass gear doesn't apply. Reason: bypass gear models careful use with your own kit.
- **Chat:** "{target} is dosed with {drug}."

### D2. Lingering clouds

**At the table.** A thrown Stellar Mist bomb bursts into a 20-foot cloud for 1 minute. Everyone inside makes a DC 15 Constitution saving throw or is dosed. A creature that enters the cloud or starts its turn there saves again, once per turn.

**How.** Item data plus two gate rules (D9); no new listener.
- Stellar Mist gets a "Throw as a gas bomb" save activity: range 30 feet, 20-foot sphere, 1 minute, Con DC 15, the dose marker. It carries Midi's region behavior: on entry and at turn start, use a second save activity, once per turn.
- The second activity ("Breathe the cloud") spends nothing and is marked automation-only, so it doesn't show on the sheet as a separate thing to click.
- On V14 the template is a region. Midi attaches the behavior when the template is placed, and removes the region when its timer effect on the thrower is deleted. House Automation deletes expired effects, so the cloud clears after 1 minute.
- Every region trigger uses the activity as the thrower. The thrower isn't dosed (D1), and the ticks keep working after the last bomb is spent (D9).

### D3. Overdose damage

**At the table.** An overdose can also deal damage. Black Lift: 2d8 poison on top of Poisoned and Incapacitated for 1 minute. The chat line shows it.

**How.** The overdose settings gain a damage formula and a damage type. Blank formula means no damage. The module rolls it and applies it through dnd5e, so resistance and immunity count and a concentrating caster has to save. The Details tab's Overdose section gets two fields (mockup 3). The content checker rejects a malformed formula or an unknown damage type.

### D4. Withdrawal bites in combat

**At the table.** In combat, a character in withdrawal from a mind-altering drug takes 1d4 psychic damage at the start of each of their turns; from a performance enhancer, 1d4 poison. Outside combat nothing ticks.

**How.** A Midi `flags.midi-qol.OverTime` row on the withdrawal effect, damage only, with no save: `turn=start,damageRoll=1d4,damageType=psychic,label=Withdrawal`. Midi runs it on the GM's client during combat.

Deleting the last withdrawal effect ends the addiction (v0.9.1), so nothing may delete it early. The content checker errors on a withdrawal effect that could remove itself:
- an OverTime row with a save DC and no `saveCount`/`failCount` keep-alive, a count ending in `-`, `removeCondition`, `actionSave` or `itemName`;
- a DAE special duration;
- a DAE stacking policy of none.

## Part 2: Rule changes

### D5. Abstaining always leads to withdrawal; the save decides how long

**At the table.** Thorin (Con +5) is hooked on Moonleaf Tincture, abstains, and passes his Wisdom check. Today he rolls Constitution, passes, and stays addicted for good. A smart player wants to fail, because failing means 3 harmless days and then a cure. From now on:
- Pass the Constitution saving throw: withdrawal for half the drug's length.
- Fail: the full length.
- Either way, when withdrawal ends, the addiction ends.

**Why the change.** On 2026-09-30 we decided a pass means "no withdrawal tonight, still addicted". That assumed withdrawal hurt. It didn't, and with D10 it will. Even with teeth, a character who always passes could never quit. Recovery has to be reachable for everyone; Constitution decides how bad it gets.

**How.**
- In `runAbstainBranch`, a pass applies withdrawal at half length, a fail at full. A pure helper turns the authored length and the result into seconds; permanent stays permanent.
- A blank Withdrawal DC means full length with no save, as in v0.9.2.
- Chat lines: "{actor} grits through {drug} withdrawal (Save 18 ≥ 13): 60 hours" and "{actor} falls hard into {drug} withdrawal (Save 9 < 13): 5 days".
- Every drug description says so (mockup 4).

### D6. Overdose also when you dose while still high

**At the table.** Taking a drug while its high is still on you risks an overdose, as does dosing at your tolerance limit (as today). Two hits of Black Lift inside 10 minutes: 10% chance.

**Why.** Today the overdose roll starts only at the tolerance cap, by which point the high is mostly gone. The audit expects about 10 capped doses per overdose seen. Stacking doses is the intuitive danger, and it shows up in a session.

**How.**
- The dose pipeline checks for this drug's high on the creature before replacing it: an Altered effect from this drug that is on and hasn't run out.
- `shouldRollOverdose` gains a "still high" input that opens the roll by itself.
- A forced dose (D1) counts the same way.

### D7. Advantage from a high lasts until tolerance empties it

**At the table.** Triple-Burn's advantage on initiative holds on the second and third dose, and stops only when tolerance takes the high to nothing. Numeric bonuses still shrink as today (+2, then +1, then 0).

**How.** dnd5e advantage rows are `add 1` (disadvantage `add -1`), and any other value is ignored. Halving 1 rounds to 0, so advantage would vanish on the second dose. `attenuateChangeRows` leaves rows whose key ends in `.roll.mode` alone while the curve is above 0, and zeroes them at 0. Midi flag rows are authored as `override`, which is never scaled.

### D8. Single-use gear gets used up

**At the table.** Smoking Triple-Burn with Rolling Papers uses one paper. With a reusable pipe or inhaler ready, no paper is spent.

**How.**
- After a dose the user takes themselves passes the gear check, the module spends one use (or one item) of a ready consumable gear item. It only does this when no ready reusable gear applies to that administration.
- A pure helper picks the item (lowest id first).
- Consumable gear is ready only while it has uses or quantity left.
- Nothing is spent when the gear check is switched off or "Use anyway" was clicked.

### D9. Gear and empty-drug checks fit the new activities

- **Gear check.** It applies only to activities that dose the user. Spiking a drink needs no pipe.
- **Empty-drug block.** It applies only to activities that spend the drug. The cloud's ticks spend nothing, so they keep firing after the last bomb is thrown. The drug still stays at 0 instead of being deleted, which the cloud also needs.

## Part 3: Content pass

### D10. The 18 drugs

Shared rules:
- **Addiction saves.** Mind-altering drugs roll Wisdom; stimulants and performance enhancers roll Constitution (the Details-tab hint and the Authoring wiki already say so).
- **DCs.** Addiction DCs sit at 12 to 15, the house range. The Withdrawal DC also sets how fast tolerance builds: 12 caps at 6 doses, 13 to 15 at 5, 18 at 4.
- **Withdrawal lengths** vary by category, and a passed save halves them (D5).
- **What gets automated.** Every high and every addiction penalty is automated with dnd5e or Midi keys, and the text says exactly what is automated.
  - Conditional wording that can't be automated ("for small or beautiful things", "sight-based") moves into the flavor paragraph.
  - "Advantage (or disadvantage) on saving throws against being frightened" is a rider-status row. It applies to a save against anything that would make you Frightened, the same pattern as Dwarven Resilience.
    - The row is Midi's `flags.midi-qol.advantage.save.all` (or `disadvantage`), type `override`, value `riderStatuses.frightened`, since Midi is required.
    - Automated Conditions 5e documents the same row (`flags.automated-conditions-5e.save.advantage`), and it is the fallback if Midi's fails the live test.
- **Exhaustion.** An effect can't add a level with a plain change row: dnd5e 5.3 rebuilds the level from its own Exhaustion effect, and DAE marks that key as not working. DAE's status row (`macro.StatusEffect`, value `exhaustion`) does work: it adds the Exhaustion condition for as long as the effect lasts. That's how the stimulant crash gets its level (see the limits under Risks).
- **Poisoned.** Plain dnd5e 5.3 automates Poisoned as disadvantage on ability checks only. In a world with Automated Conditions 5e (the test world), it also gives disadvantage on attack rolls. Addiction penalties that repeat Poisoned still matter when the Poisoned coupling setting strips it.
- **Overdose** is on for all 18.

- **Changed highs.**

  - Combat Cocktail's +1d4 becomes +2, because dice never shrink with tolerance.

## Left out

- **Coating a weapon** (dnd5e enchant activities). Nobody has checked whether our marker survives the coating. It can come next, once D1 works at the table.
- **A custom on-use macro hook.** Authors can already set one with Midi's own field.
- **Midi's "+1d4 to resist" prompts on gear.** Not doing these at all: they'd apply to every Constitution save and duplicate the bypass system.
- **Letting a player roll the addiction save when someone else doses their character.** For now the GM's screen rolls it.
- **Per-drug icons, per-drug visual effects, and the authoring tutorials.**

## Risks we accept

- **A dosed character the thrower doesn't own** has their addiction save rolled on the GM's screen. With no GM online, dosing them does nothing (DAE needs a GM to write to them).
- **Midi's "apply and leave the button" mode** keeps dnd5e's Apply button on the card, so a second click doses twice. The live test checks the world's setting; the docs recommend Midi's other apply modes.
- **Clouds.** "Once per turn" only works in combat, and for player tokens Midi can't record it, so walking out and back in can trigger again. Two bombs from one character share one timer.
- **Poison immunity** turns poison overdose damage and poison withdrawal ticks into nothing. That's correct; it's in the docs.
- **The crash's Exhaustion** comes from DAE's status row, which adds the condition only if the character doesn't already have it. A character already at Exhaustion 2 gets no extra level from the crash. If another source raises the level during the crash, the whole Exhaustion effect may go when the crash ends; the live test checks what happens.
- **Frightened riders** only count for a save against something that applies Frightened, such as a Midi save activity. A save made straight from the sheet doesn't know what it's against.
